Fair Access Ticket Lottery · problem → prototype → comparison
The problem we're solving A product-design hypothesis, not a proven outage analysis

A concert ticket shouldn't depend on who refreshes fastest.

When a popular show goes on sale, fans may converge on checkout at the same moment. That creates a stressful race: uncertain access, pressure to keep refreshing and no clear second chance if you arrive late. How might we make high-demand booking more predictable and fair without changing the familiar checkout?

Why the current rush feels broken
01 /Everyone arrives together. Demand can concentrate into traffic spikes.
02 /Speed feels like the only advantage. Fans worry about refreshing, delays and bots.
03 /Late arrivals lack a clear fallback. Missing the rush creates uncertainty.
CURRENT: everyone tries to book at once → PROPOSED: verify → assigned slot → standby Design goal: a fairer chance, a calmer entry, a clear next step
